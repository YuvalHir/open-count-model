# data-raw

Everything here is data of the Central Elections Committee (ועדת הבחירות המרכזית), not our work.
`live/` holds unmodified copies of https://media26.bechirot.gov.il/files/expb.csv and expc.csv, gzip-compressed and
timestamped by us (UTC); `polls.jsonl` is our own poll log. Source: https://votes26.bechirot.gov.il.
The CEC owns the data and its terms apply. Not affiliated with or endorsed by the CEC. The repo's MIT license covers
the code only, not this data. Historical files are downloaded by `scripts/fetch_history.py` from data.gov.il (terms: https://data.gov.il/terms), not stored here.
