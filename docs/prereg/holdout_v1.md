# Pre-registration holdout-v1: the frozen method (tag method-v2) on elections it has never seen. Written 30.10.2026 ~00:18 IST, before running.
Data: official CEC per-polling-station results for Knesset 18, 19, 20 from data.gov.il (dataset votes-knesset, publisher: Central Elections Committee, license on the portal "Other (Open)"; the same source and script as K21-25: scripts/fetch_history.py). These files sat in data-raw/cec_history but no model result for pairs K18->19 (T=19), K19->20 (T=20) or K20->21 (T=21) has ever been computed. They are a TRUE HOLDOUT for everything frozen at method-v2 (no tuning on them: lambda=5 and all rules are from development on K22-25).
Differences from development pairs (limits, stated now): no address layer for K18-20, so matching uses the rules without addresses (the frozen "fill" rule); no seat-sharing agreements are applied (as for K22-24); seat numbers are OUR calculation, not official allocations; the K19 and K20 stations are older (more renumbering is possible; matched-vote share will be reported).
Run: frozen backtest (model/backtest.py logic) for T=19, 20, 21 with orders random (3 seeds), small_first, large_first, low_turnout_first, low_likud_first, high_likud_first, counted 1/2/5/10/20/40/60/80%. Methods: ridge (the model) and raw counted total.
Pre-registered predictions: (a) random order, 5% counted: model has fewer seats out of place than raw on each of the three pairs; (b) low-Likud-first, 20% counted: model better than raw on each pair; (c) model share MAE at 20% counted, random order, within the development range (0.14-0.23 pp) +/- 50%. Failure of any is reported as failure.
Exploratory, NOT adopted whatever happens: the rejected challenger "transfer_k002" (round2-v1) on T=20 and T=21 only (T=19 would need K17, which is an XLS not in the loader); reported as a replication of its early-help lead and of its random-order loss.
