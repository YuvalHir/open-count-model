# Pre-registration cbs-v1: CBS locality socio-economic index as extra ridge features. Written 30.10.2026 ~00:24 IST, before running.
Source: CBS (הלמ"ס) "אפיון יחידות גאוגרפיות וסיווגן לפי הרמה החברתית-כלכלית של האוכלוסייה בשנת 2021", per-locality layer on the CBS GIS portal (ArcGIS service of ISRAEL_CBS_GIS, 1195 localities with CLUSTER_2021, INDEX_VALUE_2021, population 2021). License check: CBS usage license (https://www.cbs.gov.il/he/Pages/רישיון-שימוש.aspx) allows commercial and non-commercial use with attribution; data as facts are not covered by copyright; bans re-identification of people (not relevant: locality level). Attribution: "מקור: הלמ״ס, אפיון יחידות גאוגרפיות לפי הרמה החברתית-כלכלית 2021". The raw layer is fetched by scripts/fetch_cbs_index.py and not committed (same as CEC history).
Coverage by locality code: 99.5% of valid votes (K22, K25), 94.0% in K19 (K19 file has a locality row for external envelopes etc.). Unmatched localities get the vote-weighted mean.
Caveat (stated now): the 2021 index is later than K19-K24; it is used as a static trait of the locality, not as a forecast input of its time. A small leak in time order, labelled.
Test: add cbs_index (z-score) and log(population 2021) as two extra columns to the ridge features of the frozen model (method-v2), nothing else changes. Development T=22..26 and holdout T=19..21 (separately reported). Orders random (3 seeds), low_likud_first, small_first, low_turnout_first; counted 1/5/20/40%.
Success rule (fixed now): >= 10% lower share MAE in biased orders at 5% AND 20% counted, AND <= +5% in random order, on both development and holdout pooled over pairs. Else "no gain". No tuning after seeing results.
