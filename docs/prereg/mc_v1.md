# Pre-registration mc-v1: Monte Carlo replication of transfer, GP (round3 idea 6) and switch-cutoff-5%. Written 30.10.2026 ~01:15 IST, before running.
What existed before (facts from the code): severity-grid orders (Plackett-Luce, beta 0..8) used 3 draws per beta (seeds 100-102); switch-v1 and the headline "62 -> 28" used the deterministic orders low_likud_first (1 draw, tie-break noise only, seed 0), small_first and low_turnout_first (1 draw each) and random (3 seeds). So the headline numbers were means over elections of single order runs (random: 3).
Replication: 200 NEW draws (seeds 1000..1199, not used before) of the Plackett-Luce arrival order for beta in {0, 2, 4, 8} (beta 0 = random order), counted 1%, 5%, 20%, for each election: dev T=23,24,25,26; holdout T=20,21. Methods: base (method-v2), transfer (kappa 0.02, as round2), gp (round4 idea 6, fixed hyperparameters), switch-cutoff-5% (transfer if f<=5% and g above the 95th percentile of random draws, else base; flags and thresholds as switch-v1).
Report per draw-set: mean over draws, and the 5th-95th percentile range over draws of the election-mean (dev and holdout separate), share MAE and seats out of place; and per election. Fraction of draws in which the method beats base (win rate). Success of replication: the sign and rough size of the earlier headline results hold: transfer/gp/switch gain >= 20% (mean) at beta 4 and 8 at 1% and 5%, win rate >= 80% there; and cost in random order (beta 0) reported as is. No tuning, no change of method.
